About

About Me

I’m Salma Elgezawy, an AI/ML Engineer with a background in Artificial Intelligence and experience across machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, NLP, time-series forecasting, scientific machine learning, predictive maintenance, and Digital Twins.

My work combines data-driven modeling with experimentation and real-world engineering. During my research internship at Nile University’s Nanoelectronics Integrated Systems Center (NISC), I worked on chaotic systems, reservoir computing, and physics-informed machine learning, exploring how data-driven and physics-informed methods can improve forecasting of complex dynamical systems.

Across my projects, I focus on understanding the problem and the data first, then selecting, evaluating, and optimizing the approach that best fits the task.
